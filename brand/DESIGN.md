# Silly Willy Apps: brand kit design

Agreed in conversation on 2026-11-13.

## Why
Silly Willy Apps is Paul's hobby label for his own apps and games. He works by day as a software engineer at an education technology company and makes these after hours, free of enterprise process. He misses games that had a beginning and an end, before ads and season passes cluttered the screen. Silly Willy is about bringing back that joy: fun and creativity together. It's a hobby, not a movement. Success means someone enjoys what he made.

## Goals
- A wordmark and a sticker that read as "a kid drew it on paper", with a hint of sophistication.
- A voice that's warm, a little self-mocking and friendly for kids.
- A one-page brand guide plus ChatGPT prompts for the final logo art.

## Non-goals
- No mascot. "Silly Willy" is just a fun rhyme.
- No website yet. That's the next project, built on this kit.
- No restyling of Dog Stories. Each app keeps its own look and carries only the "a Silly Willy app" stamp.

## Anti-goals
- Anything that sounds like a crusade or a manifesto.
- Edgy humour. It sits next to a kids' game, and "willy" is UK/AU slang, so the jokes stay wholesome.
- Art drawn in code. Final art comes from ChatGPT; code mockups are stand-ins only.

## Decisions (made by Paul)
- Logo system: **wordmark plus sticker** (the blend of directions C and D).
  - Wordmark: a soft, chunky serif with letters that won't stay in line, and a yellow crayon scribble behind "Silly". Used in the site header, the blog and calm places.
  - Sticker: "Silly Willy" hand-lettered on a crooked, hand-cut yellow badge with a chunky navy offset shadow, and "apps & games" in italic. Used for the app icon, social avatars, the favicon and the "a Silly Willy app" stamp.
  - Monogram: "SW" on a small sticker for tight spaces.
- Money: **no ads, ever.** Small optional purchases (like Dog Stories' +50 Energy) are fine, and so is pay-once-play-it-all.

## Suggestions (Claude's calls, open to change)
- Palette: ink navy #1F2A44, crayon yellow #FFD84D, coral #E8604C, paper cream #FBF6EA.
- Mockup fonts: Fraunces (soft, wonky serif), Caveat Brush (hand lettering), DM Sans (body). They stand in for ChatGPT's hand-drawn art, and Fraunces and DM Sans may also serve as the site's real web fonts.
- Promise line: "No ads. Just fun things I like making."
- Tagline ideas: "Apps & games, the way they used to be fun." / "Seriously un-serious apps." / "Made after hours, made with joy."
- About Me: a short story, not a CV. Games that ended, the day job (kept vague), what he makes at night, and a thank-you to players.

## Approaches considered
- **One-page brand guide + prompt sheet** (chosen): easy to see and share, and right-sized for a hobby.
- Formal brand book: overkill.
- Build the website now: it would lock in the look before the real hand-drawn logo exists.

## Deliverables
- `brand/index.html`: the one-page brand guide.
- `brand/CHATGPT_PROMPTS.md`: the three logo prompts, with where each file goes (`brand/logo/`).

## Context
- `dogstories.sillywillyapps.com` is already a static GitHub Pages site (`~/github_projs/dog-stories-site`). The main site will likely follow the same pattern.
