# Silly Willy logo art: ChatGPT prompts

Paste each prompt into ChatGPT on its own. Save the result to the path shown, as a PNG. The brand guide (`brand/index.html`) picks the files up by name and shows them in place of the code mockups.

Colours used in every piece:

| Name | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Ink navy | `#1F2A44` | letters, outlines, shadow |
| Crayon yellow | `#FFD84D` | scribble and sticker badge |
| Coral | `#E8604C` | small accents only |
| Paper cream | `#FBF6EA` | background behind the art (not in the PNG) |

---

## 1. Wordmark → `brand/logo/wordmark.png`

> Create a logo wordmark that reads exactly "Silly Willy" (two words, capital S and capital W, nothing else). Style: it looks like a talented kid drew it on paper, but with a hint of sophistication. The letters are a soft, chunky, rounded serif, hand-drawn in ink navy #1F2A44, and they don't quite stay in line: some tilt a few degrees, some sit a little high or low, like they're bouncing. Behind the word "Silly" only, add a quick, loose diagonal scribble in crayon yellow #FFD84D with a waxy crayon texture, like someone coloured it in fast and went outside the lines. No other colours, no mascot, no shadows, no extra words, no frame. Transparent background. Wide landscape, about 2400 × 800 pixels, with a little empty space around the letters.

## 2. Sticker → `brand/logo/sticker.png`

> Create a sticker logo. A slightly crooked, hand-cut rounded rectangle badge in crayon yellow #FFD84D, with a thick wobbly ink navy #1F2A44 outline and a chunky solid ink navy offset shadow down and to the right, like a cut-paper sticker on a school folder. The whole badge is tilted about 3 degrees anticlockwise. On the badge, hand-lettered in ink navy, it says "Silly Willy" in a bold, playful brush-pen handwriting, and below it, smaller, "apps & games" in an elegant hand-written italic. The lettering looks drawn by hand, not typed, with a hint of sophistication. Optional: one tiny coral #E8604C doodle accent (a small star or squiggle) in a corner. No other text, no mascot, no gradients. Transparent background outside the sticker. About 1600 × 900 pixels.

## 3. Monogram → `brand/logo/monogram.png`

> Create a small square sticker icon matching a "Silly Willy" brand. A slightly crooked, hand-cut rounded square in crayon yellow #FFD84D, with a thick wobbly ink navy #1F2A44 outline and a chunky solid ink navy offset shadow down and to the right. In the centre, the letters "SW" hand-lettered in bold, playful ink navy brush-pen handwriting, large and easy to read at a small size. The square is tilted about 3 degrees. No other text or decoration. Transparent background. 1024 × 1024 pixels, with the sticker filling about 80% of the canvas.

## If it comes back wrong

- **Wrong letters** ("Silly Wily", extra words): reply "Exactly the letters S-i-l-l-y W-i-l-l-y, nothing else."
- **White or cream background:** reply "Make the background fully transparent."
- **Too polished or too computer-made:** reply "More hand-drawn: wobblier lines, uneven letters, as if drawn with a marker on paper."
- **Too messy:** reply "Keep it hand-drawn, but cleaner and more confident, like a designer imitating a kid."
- **Colours drift:** repeat the hex codes. ChatGPT often pushes the yellow towards orange.
- Keep the version you like best for each piece and rename it to the path above.
