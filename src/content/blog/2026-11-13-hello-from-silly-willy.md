Hi! I'm Paul, and this is Silly Willy Apps: the little label for the apps and games I make after hours.

I grew up with games that had a beginning and an end. You played them, you finished them, and nobody interrupted you halfway through to sell you a season pass. I'd like to make things that feel like that again. Fun, clean, a bit silly, and actually useful.

## What you'll find here

About every two weeks I'll post something:

- **Dev stories.** How a feature came together, what broke along the way, and the occasional bug I'm a little proud of.
- **New apps.** When something new is ready, you'll hear about it here first.

If you'd like to follow along, there's an [RSS feed](/rss.xml).

## The first one: Dog Stories

[Dog Stories](https://dogstories.sillywillyapps.com) is a merge game full of heartwarming stories about dogs and their people. Merge bones and toys, scoop the occasional poop (sorry), and unlock chapters about a Golden Retriever's first day home, a Corgi who sings with the school band, and a lot more.

## The one promise

No ads. Ever. Some apps might have a small optional purchase, and some you'll just buy once and play it all. But nothing will ever pop up over the fun.

Thanks for stopping by. Really.

— Paul
